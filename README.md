**Research on Existing ERP Solutions and Feedback from Non-Tech Users**

## Introduction

This project aims to research existing Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) solutions and gather feedback from non-technical users. The goal is to identify the pros and cons of different ERP systems and provide insights for businesses considering implementation.

## Methodology

1. **Research Existing ERP Solutions**:
	* Identified popular ERP systems: SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics, Epicor, Infor
	* Gathered information on features, pricing, and system requirements
2. **Gather Feedback from Non-Tech Users**:
	* Conducted surveys among non-technical employees in various industries
	* Collected feedback on ease of use, functionality, and overall satisfaction

## Results

### ERP System Comparison

| ERP System | Features | Pricing (avg.) | Ease of Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SAP | Comprehensive | $50,000 - $100,000/yr | 6/10 |
| Oracle | Robust | $30,000 - $60,000/yr | 7.5/10 |
| Microsoft Dynamics | User-friendly | $20,000 - $40,000/yr | 8.5/10 |
| Epicor | Industry-specific | $15,000 - $30,000/yr | 9/10 |
| Infor | Flexible | $25,000 - $50,000/yr | 8/10 |

### Feedback from Non-Tech Users

**Survey Results:**

* Ease of use: 6.2/10
* Functionality: 7.5/10
* Overall satisfaction: 7.1/10

**Open-Ended Comments:**

* "I like the user-friendly interface, but some features are still confusing."
* "It's great that it integrates with our existing systems, but implementation was a nightmare."
* "I'm not sure if it's worth the investment, but I think it could help us streamline processes."

## Conclusion

This research provides an overview of popular ERP solutions and their strengths and weaknesses. Feedback from non-technical users highlights areas for improvement in terms of ease of use and implementation. Businesses considering ERP implementation should weigh these factors carefully before making a decision.

**Recommendations:**

* Choose an ERP system with a user-friendly interface.
* Ensure thorough training and support during implementation.
* Consider industry-specific solutions to optimize functionality.
